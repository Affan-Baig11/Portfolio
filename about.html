About Me

My journey into cybersecurity started with a Bachelor's in Computer Science. While software engineering taught me how to build systems, I became increasingly fascinated by how to protect, break, and secure them. This pivot led me to dive deep into network security, earning my CCNA, and transitioning my focus fully toward infrastructure and cybersecurity.

I have gained practical industry experience through internships spanning aviation, fintech, and industrial sectors. I believe that a strong security posture isn't just about implementing tools, but deeply understanding the underlying networks and systems you are defending.

What's next: I am actively pursuing my Security+ and ISC2 CC certifications to solidify my foundational cybersecurity knowledge. In the near future, I am headed to Germany to pursue a Master of Science in Cybersecurity to further specialize in this field.
